Methodology & sources
Simplified, illustrative model — not a forecast. Assumes the selected countries' emissions would otherwise hold flat at 2024 levels, declining in a straight line to the target year and staying flat after. Avoided warming applies IPCC AR6’s transient climate response to cumulative emissions (TCRE): a likely range of 0.27–0.63°C per 1,000 Gt CO₂, central estimate ≈0.45°C. The dashed 1.5°C-aligned reference is the global IPCC benchmark — a straight-line decline to net-zero CO₂ by 2050 — applied uniformly to the selected group; equity-based national pathways differ. Real trajectories also depend on non-CO₂ gases, carbon-cycle feedbacks, and what every other country does.
